E. Comparison Paragraph (for related-work discussion)
   Compared with YuTC25, this scheme keeps Tag-IMHT
indexed authentication but adds cross-file batch folding and
single-point opening. Compared with ZhangTPDS23, this
scheme preserves batch-auditing efficiency while explicitly
authenticating challenged tags/indices via Tag-IMHT. The
remaining linear factor mainly comes from authenticated evi-
dence, which is reduced by multi-proof.
